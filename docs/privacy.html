Musick Privacy Policy

YouTube account connection

Musick uses YouTube API Services. If you connect your account, Musick opens Google’s sign-in page in your browser. You can choose which account to authorize. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Information used and why

  • Musick displays the connected YouTube channel name in Settings.
  • When you import playlists, Musick reads their names and IDs, and reads video IDs, titles, channel names and thumbnail URLs to build the imported lists.
  • When you like or unlike a YouTube song through Musick, the app sends its video ID and your rating choice to YouTube to update the rating on the connected account.

Storage and transmission

The connected channel name and protected OAuth tokens are stored in Musick’s local database on your PC. Imported lists and their metadata are also stored locally. Musick uses the tokens in requests to Google’s OAuth service and YouTube Data API to maintain the connection and perform the features above. They are not sent to a Musick server for those features.

YouTube playback uses YouTube’s embedded player. Google can receive player requests and use cookies or similar storage according to its policies.

Disconnecting and removing data

In Musick, open Settings → Accounts → YouTube and sign out to remove the locally saved YouTube session. To revoke the permission on Google’s side, remove Musick under Google Account connections. Imported lists are stored separately. Remove them from Musick’s library if you want to delete those copies.
